Quantum Field Theory and Combinatorics

University of Waterloo Combinatorics and Optimization (Masters) Waterloo, Canada
Email Scholar

I’m a master's student at the University of Waterloo in the department of Combinatorics and Optimization under the supervision of Rowan Yeats. I did my undergrad in mathematical physics at the University of Waterloo. Recently, I’ve been working on combinatorial aspects of scattering amplitudes, cosmological correlators, and building computational pipelines to study the meromorphic continuation of Euler–Mellin integrals.

I am interested in studying fundamental physics through modern mathematical and computational techniques.

Research Interests

  • Scattering amplitudes
  • Cosmological correlators
  • Feynman/Euler-Mellin integrals
